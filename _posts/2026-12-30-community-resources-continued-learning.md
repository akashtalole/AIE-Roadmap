## Why Community Matters More in a Fast-Moving Field

December 22's sustainable-learning post noted that no single person can track everything — a good community effectively distributes that tracking across many people, surfacing what's actually worth your attention faster than solo monitoring ever could, and providing the practitioner perspective (December 23's preference for practitioner content over raw papers) that's hardest to get any other way.
